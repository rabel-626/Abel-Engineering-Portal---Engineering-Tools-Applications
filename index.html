About Abel Engineering

Practical engineering tools built around real production work.

Abel Engineering is a growing collection of browser-based utilities intended to make industrial engineering studies easier to perform, review, and share. The emphasis is on practical workflows: capture useful production data, turn observations into measurable information, and make the results easier to act on.

Production Focused

Tools are centered on manufacturing studies such as downtime, cycle time, labor content, takt planning, material flow, and related operational analysis.

Accessible by Design

Applications are delivered through the browser and GitHub Pages so they can be opened, demonstrated, and shared without a traditional software installation.

Built to Evolve

The portal provides one consistent entry point as additional engineering utilities, analyzers, and planning tools are developed and published.
