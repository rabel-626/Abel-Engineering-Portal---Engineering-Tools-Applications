About Abel Engineering

Purpose & Use

A growing collection of practical browser-based utilities intended to support manufacturing studies, engineering analysis, planning, and continuous-improvement work.

Purpose

Production-Focused Tools

Abel Engineering tools are built around practical workflows such as downtime observation, cycle-time and labor studies, standard-work analysis, takt planning, and material-flow evaluation.

Design Philosophy

Portable & Transparent

The suite favors browser-based tools, readable data exports, simple deployment, and version-controlled supporting documentation.

Documentation

Owned by Each Tool

Each tool keeps its work instructions, references, limitations, and data notes beside the application. The portal reads those documents dynamically through a standardized manifest.

Development Status

Actively Evolving

Applications may change as workflows are tested and refined. Important study records should preserve the applicable tool/version and source files when traceability matters.

Engineering Use Disclaimer

General intended-use language for this portal and tool suite.

Abel Engineering tools are provided as engineering analysis and planning aids. Results depend on user-entered data, observations, assumptions, configuration, and source information and should be independently reviewed before being used for production, staffing, financial, regulatory, equipment-design, or safety-related decisions.

These tools do not replace professional engineering judgment, applicable standards, manufacturer requirements, site procedures, required safety reviews, quality systems, ergonomic analysis, or regulatory obligations. Users are responsible for verifying calculations, inputs, outputs, and suitability for their intended application.

These applications are not intended to function as machine safety systems, safety PLC logic, protective devices, or real-time process-control systems.

Data & Privacy

Data-handling behavior can differ by tool. Review the individual tool's Data & Privacy document before using production, employee, customer, or otherwise controlled information. The testbench is designed so those statements live with the tool rather than being hard-coded into the portal.
